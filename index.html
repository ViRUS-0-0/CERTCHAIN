CertChain

"A decentralized platform for secure, tamper-proof credential issuance and verification using Ethereum blockchain."

Problem Statement

Digital certificates are currently prone to forgery, difficult to verify instantly, and lack a unified global standard for validation.

Literature Review / Market Research

Traditional systems rely on centralized databases which are single points of failure and slow to update.

Research Gap / Innovation

We utilize Ethereum Smart Contracts to create immutable, cryptographically secured records that are instantly verifiable from anywhere in the world.
